Lemon Meringue
Masterpiece

Our most celebrated recipe: a perfect harmony of tart citrus curd and cloud-like sweet meringue atop a buttery, flaky crust.

Prep Time

45 Mins

Servings

8 People

Rating

4.9 / 5.0

The Ingredients

The Foundation

  • 1 Pre-baked 9-inch Crust

    Shortcrust or graham cracker for the perfect snap.

Lemon Curd Filling

  • 1 cup White Sugar

    Granulated sugar provides the base sweetness.

  • 3 tbsp Cornstarch

    The essential thickening agent for a firm slice.

  • 1 1/2 cups Water

    Filtered water to carry the citrus notes.

  • 2 Large Lemons

    Both zest and juice for an intense, tart kick.

  • 3 Egg Yolks

    Beaten well to add richness and deep golden color.

  • 1 tbsp Butter

    Unsalted butter adds a silky, professional finish.

The Meringue Topping

  • 3 Egg Whites

    Room temperature whites for maximum volume.

  • 6 tbsp Sugar

    Added slowly to create stable, glossy peaks.

  • 1/4 tsp Cream of Tartar

    The secret to a meringue that never collapses.

  • 1/2 tsp Vanilla

    A hint of aroma to balance the sweet clouds.

Instructions

01

Prepare Filling Base

In a medium saucepan, whisk together sugar, flour, cornstarch, and salt. Stir in water, lemon juice, and lemon zest. Cook over medium-high heat, stirring constantly, until mixture comes to a boil.

02

Tempering & Thickening

Stir in butter. Place egg yolks in a small bowl and gradually whisk in about 1/2 cup of hot sugar mixture. Whisk egg yolk mixture back into remaining sugar mixture. Bring to a boil and continue to cook while stirring constantly until thick.
